MONZERO STUDIO — LAUNCH CHECKLIST

Design in Studio, practise on Solana Devnet for free, then mint on Solana Mainnet from your own wallet.

[ ] Confirm I made the artwork or have permission to use it.
[ ] Export the final artwork, remove location data and keep it under 25 MB.
[ ] Review names, descriptions, traits and the licence.
[ ] Check the kind, edition size or fixed token supply and decimals.
[ ] If grouping NFTs, mint the collection first, paste its Solana mint address into each member, and verify membership on chain afterwards with a Solana tool.
[ ] Host the final image and metadata; arrange pinning or ongoing hosting.
[ ] Verify the exact hosted metadata and image against their expected hashes.
[ ] Keep backups of artwork, metadata, hashes and project backups outside the browser.
[ ] Mint once on Solana Devnet and check the result in a Solana explorer before using Mainnet.
[ ] After minting, share the mint address, metadata hash and Verify link. Never share seeds or passwords.
