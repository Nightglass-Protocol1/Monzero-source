Monzero: project objectives

Where Monzero (XMZ) is going over the next six months, what the community can help with, and how we will measure progress. Published 3 November 2026 and updated as work ships.

Targets marked to be decided are proposals, not commitments. Nothing here is investment advice or a promise of value; XMZ has no market price and the code has not been independently audited.

Mission

Private, mined, community-run digital cash that people can hold, spend and trade without handing their coins to anyone: their own keys, their own node if they want one, and trades settled by atomic swaps rather than exchanges or custodians.

Principles we keep

No custody

The DEX never holds anyone's coins. No wrapped tokens, no bridges, no pooled funds.

Mined, not sold

No premine, no ICO, no token sale. Founder holdings are disclosed and bound by public commitments (no selling for 12 months, then at most 2% a month, never into a new market).

Prove it, then ship it

Every feature passes test networks first, then a small real-funds test by the project, then a gated public release with limits.

Open and verifiable

All source is public; releases are signed; evidence of tests is published. We never post price talk.

Where we are (3 November 2026)

  • doneMainnet since 25 August; stable v1.0.0 with LWMA difficulty since block 21,000 (31 October).
  • doneWallets: desktop (Windows, Linux), Android wallet and miner, CLI, and the signed web wallet at wallet.monzero.org.
  • doneMining pool with hourly payouts, public nodes, explorer, faucet, community forum.
  • doneDEX: first real-funds XMZ ↔ BNB atomic swap on mainnet (31 October); exchange-style trading screen; wallet connections for BNB, BTC, LTC and BCH.
  • doneDEX: BTC/XMZ passes every public-testnet gate with light wallets on both sides (no Bitcoin node needed); Bitcoin mainnet mode built behind a signed safety switch.
  • in progressFirst real-funds BTC/XMZ swap on mainnet (lock sent; refund recovery running after a test-tool fault, then a retry).
  • in progressMicrosoft Store edition of the wallet (no mining), resubmitted for certification.

Now (next 2–3 weeks)

DEX: Bitcoin mainnet proven

  • Complete the BTC refund recovery, then a successful real-funds BTC ↔ XMZ swap.
  • Publish the evidence (transaction IDs, both chains) on the roadmap.

Wallet distribution

  • Microsoft Store approval (Microsoft-signed Windows wallet).
  • Google Play / F-Droid submission for the Android wallet.

Network health

  • Grow independent miners and public nodes; publish a "run a node" guide push.
  • Keep founder mining below a quarter of hashrate (public commitment).

Security groundwork

  • Publish the community pentest kit: test plan, scripts and reporting rules.
  • Private reporting via security@monzero.org stays the only channel for vulnerabilities.

Next (1–2 months)

DEX public beta (gated)

  • In-app offer taking: post and take offers from the trading screen.
  • Public build with small per-trade and daily limits, controlled by a signed switch that can pause new trades.
  • Pairs: BTC and BNB first, then LTC and BCH.
  • 1% project fee on the non-XMZ side, paid to published fee addresses.

Community security testing

  • Run the pentest kit with community testers on test networks and small real trades.
  • Bug rewards paid from DEX fees once fees exist to be decided.

Wallet features

  • Web wallet: address book, payment requests, clearer sync status.
  • Light-server picker in the desktop app for DEX traders.
  • Store wallet: sharper display on high-resolution screens.

Later (3–6 months)

More markets

  • XMR route (XMZ → BTC → XMR) without custody.
  • BNB/ETH swap contract v2 with the project fee built in.

Reach

  • iOS wallet (research first; App Store rules apply).
  • Exchange listings that meet the criteria below.

Assurance

  • Independent security review when the community can fund it.
  • Reproducible builds verified by people outside the project.

Community & marketing

Moderators and marketers: ready-to-post copy, key messages, FAQ answers, scam warnings and visuals are in the community marketing kit.

Steady, honest updates

  • X: daily automated posts plus progress posts when something real ships.
  • Telegram: a weekly summary of what was built and tested.
  • Bitcointalk and the forum: release notes and the monthly report.

Explain the technology

  • Short guides: how atomic swaps work, why no custody matters, how to verify a release.
  • Screen recordings of real swaps and the web wallet.
  • Translations of the website and guides by community members.

Grow participation

  • Mining guides for desktop, servers and Android.
  • Ambassador roles for people who run nodes, translate or help newcomers.
  • Regular community calls or AMAs on the forum.

What we will not do

  • No paid promotion, price predictions or "moon" posts.
  • No token on other chains, Pump.fun or similar; any such token is not Monzero.
  • No promises of returns.

Airdrops & community rewards

Monzero has no premine and no treasury created by the protocol, so any airdrop has to come from a disclosed community pool of already-mined XMZ, with every transfer published. Rewards go to things that make the network stronger, not to sign-ups alone.

Proposed reward tracks

  • Node runners: monthly rewards for public nodes that stay online (signed node check-ins already exist).
  • DEX testers: rewards for test-network swaps and accepted bug reports during the beta.
  • Builders and translators: rewards for merged code, guides and translations.
  • Faucet: keeps giving small amounts so newcomers can try the wallets.

Rules

  • One account per person, using the existing faucet accounts (email + authenticator); duplicate accounts forfeit rewards.
  • Fixed per-person caps; amounts and recipients' public addresses published after each round.
  • Rewards are for work done, never for buying or holding.
  • Participants follow their own local laws.

To decide

  • Size and source of the community pool to be decided.
  • First round date and which tracks run first to be decided.
  • How it fits the founder commitments (a gift to the pool is not a sale).

Exchange listings: the criteria

  • Hashrate spread out enough that no single party controls the chain (founder below a quarter, pool share falling).
  • Enough independent public nodes that the network does not depend on the project's servers to be decided: number.
  • The DEX live, so XMZ can always be traded without custody.
  • Only exchanges that support privacy coins and do not demand paid listing fees we cannot justify.
  • The founder never sells into a new market (public commitment).
