    How swapping works

    The swap runs in your own Monzero wallet, and your keys never leave it — no account, no sign-up. For ETH and BNB you can also trade from this page with MetaMask: the page is only the screen, and the wallet asks you to approve every trade.

    1. Open the Monzero app. It’s already your XMZ wallet — nothing to connect.
    2. Choose a swap (say XMZ → Bitcoin). The XMZ side is ready; for the other coin, use the app’s built-in wallet or link your own — e.g. MetaMask for Ethereum-family coins.
    3. Confirm. The app performs the swap automatically across both chains.
    4. Done. The other coin lands in your wallet. If a trade can’t complete you’re automatically refunded — it can never run off with your coins.

    Works with: your Monzero wallet — plus MetaMask, Ledger or WalletConnect for Ethereum-family coins (ETH, BNB). Receive to any address, including a Ledger.

    No custody, no KYC, and no “connect wallet” button on a web page — everything happens in the app and your keys stay with you.

    Your keys, your coins

    Common questions

    How do I connect a wallet?

    Swapping is built into the Monzero wallet — your XMZ is already there. For ETH and BNB, open the order book on this page, connect MetaMask and your wallet, and accept the connection in the wallet (it shows a code to compare). MetaMask pays the coin in; the wallet runs the swap, asks you to approve each trade and sends what is left back to MetaMask. The page never sees a key and cannot start a trade. Your keys never leave your device.

    Can I use a Ledger or MetaMask?

    For Ethereum-family coins (ETH, BNB) you can sign with MetaMask, Ledger or WalletConnect. On any coin you can receive the result straight to a Ledger address. On Bitcoin/Litecoin/XMZ a small in-app wallet does the live signing — hardware wallets can’t make the special swap signatures — so you keep the bulk cold on your Ledger.

    What does it cost?

    0.1%–0.5% on Bitcoin (smaller trades a little more), free on Litecoin, plus the normal on-chain network fee each chain charges. EVM pair pricing is announced per chain.

    Can Monzero take or freeze my coins?

    No — it’s non-custodial. No company ever holds your funds and there’s no account. Trades are atomic: either both sides get paid, or both get refunded. There is no third outcome where someone keeps everything.

    Which coins can I swap?

    XMZ to Bitcoin, Litecoin, BNB, Ethereum, and Solana — with more chains over time. Monero via a Bitcoin route is planned.

    What if a swap is interrupted?

    Reopen the app and it resumes, or refunds you automatically within the timelock. See the recovery guide — you can even recover without our app if you keep the swap data.
