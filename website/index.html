07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
dive in.

Read security notes
Is Monzero a token or its own blockchain?+
Monzero (XMZ) is a native cryptocurrency on its own independent proof-of-work blockchain. It is not an ERC-20 token or the Monero network.
Can I mine Monzero using a regular CPU?+
Yes. Monzero uses RandomX proof of work, which is designed for general-purpose CPUs. Results depend on your hardware, electricity costs, network difficulty, and whether you mine solo or through a pool. Read the mining guide ↗
Was there a premine, ICO or developer fee?+
Monzero reports no premine, ICO, or developer fee. The founder nevertheless mined substantial early blocks; those holdings are disclosed rather than omitted. Read the founder disclosure ↗
Can I trade XMZ on an exchange?+
Monzero's official listing tracker currently shows no verified operational exchange trading pair. Exchange and atomic-swap development does not mean that live trading is available. Check listing status ↗
Has Monzero been independently audited?+
Monzero is experimental software and has not reported an independent security audit. Verify software releases, understand the limitations, and never share your seed or private keys. Security disclosures ↗
