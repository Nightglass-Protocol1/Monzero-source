MARKET DATA

Markets

XMZ price

No verified market price. XMZ is not trading on any verified exchange, so there is no price, 24-hour change, volume, order book, spread or liquidity to show.

Any XMZ “price” you see elsewhere right now does not come from a verified market. Treat it with caution.

When markets open

Once an exchange listed as live on the exchanges page offers a public market-data API, this page will show its price, 24-hour change, volume, spread and depth. Each figure will name its exchange and show when it was fetched. Data that is old or comes from an unverified source will be marked unavailable rather than estimated.

The project will not create, simulate or pay for trading activity. The fundraising page explains how listing funds are reported.
