DEX opening rate

$0.01 per XMZ — one US cent

The rate the project set as the opening quote for XMZ on the Monzero DEX on 15 November 2026, raised from the first quote of $0.001 (the rate of the 31 October 2026 mainnet test, 0.01 BNB for 7000 XMZ).

It is an opening quote the project sets for its own DEX, not a market price. The price XMZ actually trades at is the NoirTrade figure above. The earlier tiny BTC/LTC tests moved fixed coin amounts to exercise the swap machinery and were never meant to express a price, so they are not used for this rate.

About the XMZ price

The XMZ figures come from NoirTrade's public market-data service and name it as their source. This site reads them at most once a minute and shows when they were read. If the exchange cannot be reached, the last figures stay on the page with their time, and are marked out of date after 15 minutes. Nothing is estimated or filled in.

The project will not create, simulate or pay for trading activity. The fundraising page explains how listing funds are reported.
