The four rules

Read these first. They protect the community and the project.

No price talk

Never predict, promise or discuss price, returns or "gains". XMZ has no market price yet. Redirect: "We talk about technology and adoption, not price."

Be accurate

Use the facts on this page and the press kit. If you are not sure, link the source instead of guessing. Say what is tested, not what is planned, as done.

Be honest about risk

The code is open source but not independently audited. The DEX is in testing. Say so when relevant; it builds trust.

Never ask for keys

No moderator or team member ever asks for a recovery phrase, private key or password, and never DMs first about support or airdrops.

Talking points

The points to make, by topic, with the page to link.

Privacy

  • Private by default, not optional.
  • RingCT hides amounts; rings of 16 hide senders; one-time addresses hide receivers.
  • Built from the battle-tested Monero codebase.

Fair mining

  • RandomX: designed for ordinary CPUs, not ASICs.
  • Pool with 1% fee and hourly payouts, or mine solo.
  • Android miner app for phones.
  • A permanent 3.26 XMZ tail reward keeps the chain secure.

No sale, full disclosure

  • No premine, no ICO, no token sale, no developer fee.
  • The founder mined early blocks while the network was tested and publishes the holding and commitments (founder statement).

Your keys, your coins

  • Web wallet: keys never leave the browser.
  • Desktop and Android wallets with your own 25-word recovery phrase.
  • Signed releases you can verify.

DEX without custody

  • Atomic swaps: either both sides get paid or both get refunded.
  • No exchange, custodian, bridge or wrapped token.
  • First real-funds XMZ ↔ BNB swap done; Bitcoin in mainnet testing.
  • Public trading opens in gated stages with small limits.

Open development

  • All code public on GitHub.
  • Test evidence published on the roadmap.
  • Clear objectives and honest status.

Moderator guide

Scam patterns to remove

  • DMs offering "support", "recovery" or "wallet sync".
  • Fake airdrops, giveaways, "double your XMZ".
  • Tokens on other chains using the Monzero name.
  • Links to look-alike domains (anything not monzero.org).
  • Impersonating admins (copied names and photos).

Price and shilling

  • Remove price predictions and pump talk.
  • Reply once with the price FAQ answer, then move on.
  • No paid promotion or referral links.

Escalate

  • Security issues: ask the reporter to email security@monzero.org privately; never discuss details publicly.
  • Lost funds: point to the recovery phrase restore guide; never ask for the phrase.
  • Press or exchange contact: point to the press kit.
