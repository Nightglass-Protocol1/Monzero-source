Join or leave

The reporter runs next to your own node and sends only a random installation ID, your hashrate, a block count and whether you are mining. Ask in the community for a reporting token. To leave, stop the reporter or delete its token file. You disappear from this page within 3 minutes, and your record is deleted after 7 days. With --share-history the service also keeps your daily totals (reports, hashrate, blocks, active 10-minute periods) for 30 days for the boards above; run the reporter without it and they are deleted at your next report.
