Engineering roadmap · Multi-asset DEX

The road to public swaps

A non-custodial atomic-swap exchange built into the Monzero app. You trade XMZ for another coin straight from your own wallet — the two blockchains enforce the trade, so either both sides get paid or both get refunded. No account, no deposit, no custodian.

Live with small limits. XMZ trades against ETH, BNB and SOL since 12 November 2026, and against USDT (on Solana and on BNB Smart Chain) since 18 November, from the Linux wallet (its own Exchange page since v1.2.0, offers open up to a month since v1.3.0), the Windows wallet beta, the DEX page and the Android wallet. Every new trade is checked against per-trade and daily limits in a document signed by the project, which can also pause new trades. The code has not had an independent security audit: trade only amounts you can afford to lose.

Status as of 19 November 2026. Milestone-based delivery; no committed dates.

Where each pair stands

The DEX is crypto-only — XMZ against Ethereum, BNB, Solana and USDT (on Solana and BNB Smart Chain) today; Bitcoin and Litecoin are built and tested but not listed yet. (Earlier Bitcoin Cash and Dogecoin work was dropped: a legacy-signature malleability issue made their pre-signed refunds unsafe.)

Monero (XMR) is in development as a trading coin against BNB, Ethereum and Solana, with XMZ/XMR as two swaps back to back through one of them. 14 Nov 2026: full swaps, refunds and restarts passed on a local Monero test chain with the official Monero 0.18.5.4 software. 15 Nov 2026: the first run on Monero's public test network (stagenet) did not finish, because the public stagenet node stopped giving blocks to the trade wallets. No swap took place; the run showed that a refund could stall while the Monero side was unreachable, which is now fixed. 21 Nov 2026: XMR pairs opened for development in the Linux wallet: XMR against ETH, BNB, SOL and USDT (on Solana and on BNB Smart Chain), each with its own order book, from a Monero wallet the app runs on the project's own Monero node; XMR/XMZ as two swaps through one of those coins. 22 Nov 2026: the repeated stagenet swap completed end to end on both sides (test ETH for stagenet XMR), on the project's own Monero node. Next: a real-funds swap of each pair. Not yet available to trade.

XMZ / BTC mainnet tested

Passed every test-network gate on signet and testnet4 — swap, refund, two machines, interruption and power-loss — with light wallets on both sides (no Bitcoin node needed). The first real-funds BTC↔XMZ swap on Bitcoin mainnet completed on 4 November. On 8 November a signet swap was started from a signed offer posted to a relay and completed through it — Bitcoin lock, XMZ lock, redemption and payout. Not listed on the public DEX yet.

XMZ / BNB live

A chain-agnostic EVM atomic-swap contract on BNB Smart Chain. Swap and refund passed on testnet; the first real-funds XMZ↔BNB swap on mainnet completed on 31 October.

XMZ / SOL live

A dedicated Solana atomic-swap program (native SOL and SPL). Deployed to Solana mainnet and verified with a live real-funds swap — lock → claim, escrow closed — on 6 November. On 12 November a SOL/XMZ swap completed on mainnet end to end through the public wallet and the DEX page with Phantom, from an offer posted to the open relays.

XMZ / LTC mainnet validating

A Litecoin light wallet on both sides. Passed the public-testnet gates; the mainnet path is now enabled and validated end-to-end in a dry run (light-wallet sync and full setup). A first real-funds swap is the next step.

XMZ / ETH live

Uses the same EVM contract as BNB, with Ethereum's finality wait (96 blocks) before either side acts. Passed every test-network gate on Sepolia — a full swap, both refund cases, and stop-and-resume of each side — then the first real-funds XMZ↔ETH swap on Ethereum mainnet completed on 8 November. Low-gas L2s such as Base and Arbitrum come later.

ETH / BNB / SOL coin for coin in development

ETH, BNB and SOL swapped directly against each other, with no XMZ in between, on the same swap contract and Solana program that are already live (no new contracts). 20 November 2026: full trades through a relay passed on local test chains, including ETH for SOL and SOL for ETH, and the trading screens were built: the desktop Exchange, and the DEX page with approval in the desktop or Android wallet. 22 November 2026: all three pairs completed end to end on public test networks, payouts included: ETH for BNB (Sepolia, BNB Smart Chain testnet), ETH for SOL and SOL for BNB (Solana devnet). Next: a real-funds swap of each pair before it opens.

XMZ / USDT live

Tether on Solana (SPL, through the Solana swap program) and on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20, through the EVM contract). Live since 18 November with the same small limits; needs Linux wallet v1.3.3, Android wallet 1.1.10, Windows wallet v1.3.3 (beta) or later.

What's next

  • Independent security review / community pentest. The swap contracts and engine have not been externally audited. Limits stay small until this is done.
  • Bitcoin and Litecoin on the public DEX. BTC/XMZ is mainnet tested and runs through the relays on test networks; LTC needs its first real-funds swap. Both need their trading flow finished in the wallets before they are listed.
  • The in-wallet Exchange on more platforms. Linux has it since v1.2.0 and Windows as a beta since 16 November; the Windows wallet still needs wider testing and a code-signing certificate. Android trades through the DEX page for now.
  • Monero (XMR) against ETH, BNB, SOL and USDT, and XMR/XMZ as two swaps: a real-funds swap of each pair (the swap on Monero's public test network passed on 22 November); the Android wallet after the Linux one.
  • Coin for coin between ETH, BNB and SOL: all three passed on public test networks (22 November); next a real-funds swap of each before it opens, with the same small limits.
  • Monzero Box. Ready-made Docker, virtual-machine and Raspberry Pi images: an XMZ node, a DEX relay shared with the network over Tor (no router setup, home IP hidden) and optionally the wallet, set up from a browser, locked down by default, and kept up to date only with releases signed by the project.
  • Low-gas L2s such as Base and Arbitrum on the existing EVM contract.
  • Raised limits, step by step by new signed documents, after weeks of clean trading.

What this roadmap means

Readiness is per market

Each pair needs its own verified settlement and recovery before it is offered. Progress on one pair is not a claim about another.

No custody, ever

The app never holds your coins. Trades are atomic — both sides settle, or both refund. Contract and program addresses are pinned in the app build, never fetched from the website.

Evidence, not promises

Source is public, releases are signed, and test evidence is published. No price talk, and no launch-date hype.
