Mining difficulty

Block intervals

Seconds between consecutive block timestamps. Individual intervals vary; negative timestamp gaps are omitted.

Transaction activity

Transactions per block, excluding the miner reward. Transactions are not unique people.

GROWTH & PRIVACY

What about new users?

Monzero does not publish a count of new wallets or unique users. Private addresses and transactions cannot provide a reliable people count. We do not collect wallet identities to create one.

The charts above show actual network activity. Opt-in active miners are reporting installations, not unique people, and exclude miners that do not participate. Historical user growth is unavailable; we will not fill it with estimated sign-ups.

Data and chart methodology

Source: the Monzero public mainnet node. Responses are checked for complete, linked headers matching the reported tip; this is source consistency checking, not independent consensus verification. Up to 720 recent blocks; the time span depends on actual block production. Refreshes every minute. Charts use block height on the horizontal axis. Hover or focus a point for its value; the expandable table below contains every sample. Network hashrate is an estimate, not a direct measurement. A single public node does not measure total network node count or decentralization.
