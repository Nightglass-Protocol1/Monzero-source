Service status

Whether the services behind this website are running. Checks refresh every minute; the owner is emailed when one changes.

Loading the status…

    What is checked

    • Public node: node.monzero.org answers and its newest block is less than 30 minutes old (blocks can be slow when little hash power is mining).
    • Mining pool: pool.monzero.org answers and is on the same height as the public node.
    • Faucet: the payout worker checked in within 10 minutes, is not halted and has funds for at least two days at the daily maximum.
    • Node map census and network history: their latest data arrived on schedule.
    • EU and US nodes: each checks in every 10 minutes.
